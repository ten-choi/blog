I kept my Duolingo streak going for about 255 days.

About half the words it showed me were ones I already knew, and half were new.

At first I did it for motivation, so I kept at it.

At some point I stopped seeing useful words. I wondered whether it was because my skills had improved.

Around the 200-day mark, I started asking myself whether this was even meaningful. It felt a bit like forcing myself to do it before bed, and it didn't seem to help my studying much.

When I hit around 254 days, I did feel a sense of accomplishment. Doing something every single day had meaning in itself.
But I also felt it had become less efficient than talking with AI.
These days, talking with AI while walking around seems to be the best option for me.
