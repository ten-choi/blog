I went to NOVA for about nine months, from February to the end of October 2025.
Partly it was to keep talking in English, and partly to keep my motivation up.
I went every Tuesday, and I was fairly satisfied with it.

Talking with native-speaker teachers was good in itself, and going consistently was good too.
But the textbook had its limits, and since it was a group lesson, the skill gap between students was sometimes huge.
That said, a "group" lesson quietly turned into a one-on-one now and then, so it was never a big problem.

Personally, I kind of like group lessons. Part of me thinks that if I'm going to do one-on-one, I might as well just talk to an AI, and a group means meeting new people.

That's my personality too: the slightly awkward air of doing something with other people makes me uncomfortable, yet I actually lean toward being around people.

That's why I stuck with it. But my level eventually went up,
and I felt that classes at this level no longer fit me. I hit a ceiling, so I decided to quit.
Around that time, I also started talking with AI a lot more.
