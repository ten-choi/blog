From April to October, I spent about six months taking English lessons with Landy.
Depending on how you look at it, that's either a long time or a short one.
I took around 74 lessons and picked up a lot of expressions I didn't know before.
Now it's time to wrap things up.

## How I Found It

I used to have calls with a Korean friend who speaks English fluently, and he's the one who introduced me to this online English service.
He only mentioned it in passing, but the name stuck with me because I was already curious about other ways to learn English. Until then, I had always gone to language schools.

## The Good Side

There were a lot of upsides.
I didn't have to commute to a language school, and I could book lessons whenever it suited me.
The tutors may not all be native speakers, but their English is close to native level.

## The Not-So-Good Side

It's not really a flaw of the service.
When a lesson is locked into a fixed schedule, I show up no matter what. With Landy, though, it was up to me to use my tickets, which made it easy to put lessons off.
For me, that turned out to be a downside...
On Hammerstein's matrix, I try to be the "smart and industrious" type. To become that type, I need to put myself inside a system that keeps me learning, like a cog in a machine.

## What I Got Out of It

Even so, it kept me motivated to study English consistently, and my range of expressions grew a lot through these lessons.
Being thrown into all kinds of situations made it easier to find the right words in unfamiliar ones.
And having to explain Korean things in English really stretched how much I can express.
Languages really are about listening and speaking, again and again. Reading and writing matter too, but I think listening and speaking come first. After all, language is a tool for communicating with people.

## What's Next

1. I've been slowly making friends to speak English with.
2. I've been looking for English social events where I can practice speaking.
3. I want to look for English classes built around discussions or meetings, and start joining them.
