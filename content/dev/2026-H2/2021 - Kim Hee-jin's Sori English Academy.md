 This was the first English academy I ever attended.

 I think I went to Sori English for about six months.
 The method is to look at sentences, repeat them, and say them out loud.

 Rather than clearly defining what I needed and planning how to get there, I instinctively felt that I needed to study English.
 I had felt it since I was a kid, but I never thought it was essential, so I just let life drift along. Then, after I started working at my first company and earning money,
 for some reason I began to feel that I should go to an English academy on my own.

So, feeling that studying alone wasn't enough, I started attending an English academy in Daejeon.
