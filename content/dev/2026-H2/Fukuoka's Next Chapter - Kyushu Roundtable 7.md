I heard there was an English-language event about the future of Fukuoka, so I decided to check it out!


## About the Event

- **Event**: [Kyushu Roundtable #7: Fukuoka's Next Chapter](https://www.fukuoka-now.com/en/event/kyushu-roundtable-7-fukuokas-next-chapter/)
- **When**: Friday, October 30, 2026, 18:30–20:00
- **Where**: CIC Fukuoka (Tenjin)
- **Hosted by**: Fukuoka Now
- **Language**: English

With the mayoral election coming up on November 15, the theme was "What should Fukuoka prioritize over the next four years?" <br>
It felt more like a conversation than a lecture, moving from recent local news to the main topic and then to comments from the audience.

## Why I Went

It was a good chance to practice my English, but more than that, I wanted to know how other people see the future of Fukuoka, the city I live in.

## What Came Up

- Junglia, the new theme park in Okinawa, came up. The message seemed to be that it hasn't paid off, so we shouldn't go down that path here.
- Concept images for turning the Hakozaki area into a tourist spot were shown, which led to a discussion about whether more tourism is a good thing.
- People in the audience shared their own concerns: public transportation, digitalization, worries about gentrification, and sudden rent increases for tenants.

## What I Wanted to Know — Tourist Destination or Business Hub?

What I really wanted to know was what the city's top priority is. <br>
Whether Fukuoka grows as a tourist destination or as a business hub changes the kind of life I can picture for myself here.<br>
Judging from the slides and the conversation, the focus seemed to lean more toward tourism than toward becoming a business hub. <br>
I was curious about the business side, but my question felt a little off-topic for where the Q&A was going, so I held back. (Next time I'll just ask!)<br>
I'm thinking about moving to Tokyo to join a bigger company. If becoming a business hub is one of Fukuoka's goals, the city would be a great place to come back to someday.

## What I Took Away

This event made me realize how many doors English opens for me. There was a lot of information I would have missed without it, and getting to meet people from completely different fields was a big plus.

If you want a richer life and more opportunities, learning English is a pretty smart investment.
