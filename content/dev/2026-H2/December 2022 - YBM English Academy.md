I went here for about two months, maybe? I honestly don't remember exactly how long I attended.
What I wanted was conversation practice, but this place felt a bit different. It wasn't the kind of English I had in mind.
There were probably around 40 people in the class?
That's a lot, and although I never defined what kind of class I wanted, I think a small group lesson was what I really needed.
The instructor was very passionate about teaching, and the approach was also close to "sound English": keep saying it out loud.
