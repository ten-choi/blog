### Why

I could never understand why we go to school and study the basic subjects.

Now that I'm older, I've figured out why. 

To cut to the chase, I didn't feel the need for it. I've also come to understand why wealthy families, or families that are passionate about education, focus so much on math and English. But in an ordinary environment, kids just pour their effort into the ordinary school subjects.

That's because when you're young, you can't tell what kind of study actually suits you.

You might not have the power to push back against the social system, or your family might not be able to give you that information.

In my personal opinion, learning how to get along with people and studying for brain development could wrap up in middle school.

Unless a family can give their kids lots of experiences from a young age and help shape their dreams,

I wish schools would teach the importance of choosing a career and focusing on it starting in high school.

The world has developed to the point where, unless you're so poor you can't even eat, I feel the opportunity to learn is open to you.

The world is unfair, and education is a privilege of those who are awake to it. 

That's the end of my rant.
