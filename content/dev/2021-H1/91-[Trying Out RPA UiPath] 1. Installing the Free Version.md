### \- Installing UiPath

Search Google for **RPA UiPath**, then click **Free trial**.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/ten-choi/blog-images@main/dev/tistory/91/img.webp)

Once you're on the page, click the **Try **UiPath** Free** button at the **top right**.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/ten-choi/blog-images@main/dev/tistory/91/img_1.webp)

There are several trials available, but we'll use the free **Community Cloud**.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/ten-choi/blog-images@main/dev/tistory/91/img_2.webp)

The site requires you to log in. I logged in with my **Google account**.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/ten-choi/blog-images@main/dev/tistory/91/img_3.webp)

After logging in, you'll land on a screen like the one below. Download **UiPath Studio**.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/ten-choi/blog-images@main/dev/tistory/91/img_4.webp)

**Run** the downloaded file. A screen like this appears and the installation proceeds.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/ten-choi/blog-images@main/dev/tistory/91/img_5.webp)

Once the installation finishes, launch **UiPath Studio**. You'll see a screen like the one below.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/ten-choi/blog-images@main/dev/tistory/91/img_6.webp)

**UiPath** is installed. Nice work. If you'd like to see an actual result

from a **UiPath** **project**, please check out the **next post**. Thanks.
