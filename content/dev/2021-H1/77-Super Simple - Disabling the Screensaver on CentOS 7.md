### **\- How to Disable the Screensaver on CentOS 7**

**1\.** First, start **CentOS 7**, click **Applications** in the menu bar, and follow the image below.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/ten-choi/blog-images@main/dev/tistory/77/img_1.webp)

**2\.** When the **Settings** screen appears, select the **Privacy** menu and click **Screen Lock**.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/ten-choi/blog-images@main/dev/tistory/77/img_2.webp)

**3.** When the **Screen Lock window** appears as below, switch it from **On** to **Off**. **But you're not done yet.**

   Turning off the **lock** here only means the screen **won't lock**, so you won't have to re-enter your password.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/ten-choi/blog-images@main/dev/tistory/77/img_3.webp)

**4.**  Click **Power** in the menu. You need to turn off **power saving** — that's what keeps the screensaver from kicking in.

    Set **Blank screen (B)** to **Never**.

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/ten-choi/blog-images@main/dev/tistory/77/img_4.webp)

If you've run into the same annoyance as I did, this is a good way to turn off the screensaver.

I hope it went smoothly. Happy coding!
