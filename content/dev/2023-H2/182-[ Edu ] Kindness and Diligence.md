If I had to pick the two things I value most in the mindset I want to carry through life,

they'd be **kindness** and **diligence**. Everyone knows them, and they're pretty hard to actually practice

### \- Splitting Kindness into Two

Situations where you can be generous usually come down to **money** and **sharing skills**

Everyday behavior can be kindness too, but I'm focusing on giving.  **Doing something without expecting anything in return**

Quite often, spending a little more money lets you avoid a **small quarrel**

Here's a simple example

When a group of friends eats out together, there's often one who steps up and offers to pay the whole bill at once

In this case, that friend was after a **tax deduction**. I knew about this too and used to step up a bit myself

As we got older, some friends started competing to be the one who pays

Small things like this have a way of turning into **arguments**

It was nothing, and it ended fine, but it made me feel I shouldn't step in on things like that

Thinking about it, it really is nothing. I wonder if it's even worth getting worked up over

On the knowledge side, it's about **sharing knowledge I worked hard to earn**

My friends in the same field are all juniors, so it often turns into a relationship where **I'm the one sharing skills, one way**

Just having that thought shows how small I can be 

In the big picture, I'm not sharing to get something back, and I'm already **sharing with the whole world through my blog**, aren't I?

I tend to expect something back without realizing it, and I want to let go of that thought entirely

### \- Splitting Diligence into Two

Do I keep **studying and blogging** in my free time?

Am I **working out twice a week**? It splits into those two

Not everyone can succeed, but I know well that among **successful people**, there's no one who didn't put in the effort

So I keep investing time in constructive things like blogging and studying development

That said, lately I've been slacking a bit, using work as an excuse

I have a friend who works really hard. If only because of them, I **need to pull myself together**

I'm mostly keeping up with working out twice a week, but sometimes it's only once

Exercise may not seem related to career achievement, but many successful people **put a high value on self-discipline**

And exercise is part of that. Let's keep this one steady

Let's try to stick to all of the above. I think if I keep at it for just **15 more years**, these worries will go away
