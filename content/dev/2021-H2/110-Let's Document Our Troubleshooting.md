#### **Topic: Why do you need to document troubleshooting**

Let's define troubleshooting as  
diagnosing and fixing an issue  
when one comes up in software.  

Why do we need it?  
So we don't have to go through the same problem twice.

Just follow the order above.  
Every time a problem comes up, go through troubleshooting in that order.

I think developers are among the people who benefit most from running into errors and documenting them.  
It's a hassle, but let's keep at it.
