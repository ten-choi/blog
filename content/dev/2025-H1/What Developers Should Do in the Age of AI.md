What developers should do -> AI keeps getting stronger

More and more, the role will center on repeating a loop of communication, product design, code review, and product QA.
The most important part of that is communication.
Anyone can do these tasks, but communication is the only one that involves human emotions.
So knowing how to get your point across without hurting the other person's feelings, and building good relationships, become even more important.
Even if the swords once forged with a hammer are now made in a high-tech gigafactory, it's still people who discuss what to do with them.
