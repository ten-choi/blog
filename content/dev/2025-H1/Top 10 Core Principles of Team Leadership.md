﻿# Core Principles of Team Leadership - Top 10 by Priority

## 1. Build Trust ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Keep even small promises, share information openly, and make your actions match your words to earn the team's trust.

## 2. Core Mindset ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Assume the problem lies in the situation or structure, not the person, and put preserving relationships first.

## 3. Psychological Safety ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
Create an atmosphere where questions and mistakes are okay, so team members can share their opinions freely.

## 4. Effective Communication ⭐⭐⭐⭐
Lead with the conclusion and the reason, have important conversations face-to-face or by voice, and use I-messages.

## 5. Conflict Prevention ⭐⭐⭐⭐
Make roles and responsibilities clear, share expectations concretely, and set team standards in advance.

## 6. Handling Conflict When It Happens ⭐⭐⭐⭐
Hold back emotional reactions (the 24-hour rule) and approach it structurally: facts, impact, cause, then resolution.

## 7. Leading Difficult Conversations ⭐⭐⭐
Don't avoid problems; handle them in private conversations, give behavior-based feedback, and offer alternatives.

## 8. Making Use of 1on1s ⭐⭐⭐
Balance talk about work and about the person, and actively find out what's causing stress and what support is needed.

## 9. Delegation and Coaching ⭐⭐⭐
Don't try to solve everything yourself; use code reviews as coaching opportunities and help the team grow.

## 10. Leader Self-Care ⭐⭐
Don't take 100% responsibility for your team members' feelings, share your concerns with peers and managers, and prevent burnout.
