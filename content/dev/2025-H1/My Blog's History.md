## The Beginning (2020, Tistory)

I started blogging because I wanted to share what I'd learned.
In college I mostly worked with Unity and C#, but after the first semester of my junior year I did an internship and started studying web development.
My development journey began with website frontends and backends.
I used Node.js back then, and while studying I leaned heavily on other people's blogs. After getting so much help from their posts, I wanted to leave a record of my own.
My first post, in May 2020, explained what Node.js is and how to install it. I've been writing a little at a time ever since.

## The Move (Medium)

At first I ran the blog on Tistory, a popular blogging platform in Korea, but it bothered me that only Korean readers would ever see it.
I wanted to share in other languages too, so I started thinking about writing in English.
Cumulative views reached about 100,000, which I was happy with, and around then I moved over to Medium.

## Rethinking It (2025, dev.to vs Blogger)

But around early 2025, Medium stopped issuing API tokens.
If a platform is that reluctant about its API, there's no telling what it'll do next, so I started reconsidering.
I weighed dev.to against Google Blogger and posted on dev.to a few times, but concluded that being locked into a platform makes customization hard.

## Now (GitHub + Blogger)

In the end, I settled on managing posts as Markdown on GitHub and publishing them to Google Blogger through its API.
I plan to bring over some posts from my old blogs too, because they're both a record and a memory.

## Why I Write Now

As a developer, AI has greatly reduced the need to document my own development work.
So these days I sometimes write less to share and more to organize my own thoughts.
I feel that building the ability to communicate well-organized opinions to others is important in its own right.
