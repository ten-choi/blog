This was my first time attending an AWS event

I only wanted the technical content, so I attended just Thursday's technical track sessions

I left from the office, so I barely made it to the first session in time  
I listened to about 5 sessions, and wrote up the 3 or so I liked

1.  A startup's data warehouse build-out
2.  Data sharing and utilization, and company-wide data governance  
    
3.  Cloud-native MSA through 12 design patterns

![](https://cdn.jsdelivr.net/gh/ten-choi/blog-images@main/dev/archive/aws-summit-seoul-2023-후기/img.webp)

  
The first session I went to was really popular  
Staff told me it was full, and I wondered why they were even asking, so I said I'd go in standing  
Turns out there were wristbands for the free lunch  
It's not really about the food, but I'm a bit bummed I missed the chance to eat a lunch from AWS

I sat through sessions all day, but later on there weren't any I really wanted, so I half-listened and then walked around the booths  
Maybe because I got there late, most of the booths were wrapping up  
I got an AWS-branded cup as a giveaway. I don't care much about the other little stuff, but I really like the cup  
If you had 3 or more AWS certifications, they gave you a T-shirt  
Too bad I missed out, but I don't think it's worth getting certified just for that
