It's fair to say my Tistory is purely a dev blog  
It looks like a dev blog made by an engineering grad purely to keep records  
Maybe because that's exactly what it was, I never thought it looked pretty  

My goal was a well-organized blog built around categories  
And I write things down so I can look back at them later, or so someone will point out my mistakes  
I want people to tell me, "Why did you write this wrong?"  

I'd been writing that way for quite a while. Then one day someone brought up the impression my blog gives off  
They said it was exactly the kind of blog I'd make  

That got me thinking a lot  
I don't know what they meant by it, but at some point this blog had become pretty precious to me  
So I didn't let a comment like that just pass me by  

I figured that since I'd grown attached to it, maybe it was time I started taking care of it  
I thought about how to redesign it, and even considered moving the blog  
I'd also seen a notice that Tistory could put in whatever ads they wanted

1.  Google Blogger
2.  GitHub blog
3.  velog

So I weighed those 3 to decide where to move. I tried each of them once  
What I felt was that it put the cart before the horse  

Of course, moving comes with trade-offs, but none of them was a good enough experience to leave Tistory over

1.  Moving a blog takes quite a bit of work (I was planning to move my old posts over bit by bit too)
2.  Tistory is friendly for writing
3.  It'd be a waste to give up my current traffic

So I decided to remodel instead of rebuilding  
I tried tweaking it myself a few times. I liked it, of course, but I had a feeling it wouldn't look good objectively..  
So I went looking for shared third-party skins  

I went back and forth between various skins and settled on the one this blog uses now  
If you're curious, the copyright link is at the bottom left  

I think it's a well-organized skin that looks good, too. I plan to stick with it for a while
