OOP: Object-Oriented Programming

In the real world, things like desks and chairs are what we call objects.  
The idea is to analyze things like chairs and desks and apply that to development.  
I want to apply it, but just saying "do object-oriented development" is vague.  
You need to define the problem precisely.  
  
OOP has five principles.  
If your code doesn't violate those five principles, you can say you're doing OOP.  
In other words, don't focus on "OOP" itself; focus on satisfying the five principles.
