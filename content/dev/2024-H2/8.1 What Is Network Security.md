# 8.1 What Is Network Security?

### Properties required for secure communication

- Confidentiality
    - Only the sender and the intended receiver should be able to understand the contents of the transmitted message.
    - Since an eavesdropper may intercept the message, it must be encrypted in some way so the eavesdropper can't make sense of it.
- Message integrity
    - The contents of the communication must not be altered in transit.
- End-point authentication
    - Each party must be able to confirm the identity of the other party in the communication.
- Operational security
    - Today, most organizations' networks are connected to the public Internet.
    - That puts them at risk of attacks from outside, so they need firewalls and other security systems to defend against them.
