Ten

Backend developer, product engineer in progress

Hi, I'm Ten, a backend developer working my way toward being a product engineer.

Most of my career has been on the server side: APIs, databases, and the infrastructure that keeps them running. Lately I care just as much about the other half of the job: understanding what users actually need, turning requirements into a design, and shipping something that solves the problem rather than just compiling.

What you'll find here

  • Backend and system design: architecture, caching, queues, and mock design interviews worked through end to end.
  • Databases: SQL tuning, execution plans, and data engineering basics.
  • AI-assisted development: what coding agents and AI tools are actually good for in day-to-day work.
  • Learning in public: networking notes, coding test practice, and the occasional post about studying English and Japanese.

About the archive

I started writing in 2020, in Korean, as a way to keep track of what I was learning. Those older posts have since been translated into English and are tagged Archive. They are early notes, so some are rough, but I've kept them as a record of how I got here. If you only want the newer writing, use Browse → Hide archive in the menu.
